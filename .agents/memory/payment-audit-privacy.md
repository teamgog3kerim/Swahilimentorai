---
name: Payment audit privacy
description: Privacy boundary for review evidence retained after permanent account deletion.
---

Retained payment reviews are minimal administrative evidence, not recoverable learner or payment profiles. Do not enrich orphaned reviews with deleted learner names, emails, transaction details, or inferred personal data.

**Why:** The requested account-deletion behavior deliberately preserves review evidence while removing the learner and original payment. Making evidence readable must not reverse that privacy boundary.

**How to apply:** Keep retained reviews admin-only and read-only. Show only existing snapshots; do not fabricate details or restore deleted account records to make history easier to display.
