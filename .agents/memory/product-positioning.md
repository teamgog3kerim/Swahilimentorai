---
name: Business communication positioning
description: Intended audience and business-oriented messaging for Ethiopanda.
---

Position Ethiopanda for Chinese- and English-speaking learners doing business
in Africa, highlighting language learning as a way to support communication
and relationships with customers and partners.

**Why:** The user described this business audience and requested professional,
concise, attractive messaging in the hero and friend invitations.

**How to apply:** Include both Amharic and Swahili and both interface languages.
Describe confidence and communication benefits without promising business
results, implying specialized business courses, or claiming these are Africa's
largest languages without evidence.
